# Babillard : accueil (`bf_babillard_home`)

A bridge between the noticeboard (`bf_babillard`) and the home dashboard (`bf_home`). It installs itself as soon as both modules are present.

A tile in the dashboard's actions required says how many required-reading announcements are still waiting for your confirmation, and shows their titles.

The tile only counts what is waiting for you, never what is waiting for someone else, so there is no leaderboard of latecomers. The list of people who have not read a post does exist, but it is for editors and lives in the post itself.
