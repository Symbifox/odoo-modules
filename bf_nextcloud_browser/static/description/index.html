Nextcloud File Browser

Your Nextcloud files and client folders, inside Odoo

A WebDAV file browser embedded on Odoo companies, contacts, projects and tasks, plus a standalone application. Each client keeps its Nextcloud folder; Odoo opens it from the record you are on, creates it from your template, and files the attachments of a conversation into it.

  • Client folders: link a company to its folder, chosen among the folders under your clients root, closest names first; its contacts, projects and tasks follow
  • Create a client folder from your template, numbered after the ones already on disk
  • File chatter attachments into the client folder: copied, kept in Odoo, never written over an existing file, and noted in the thread when the person may post there
  • Embedded Dossier Nextcloud tab on companies and contacts, Fichiers Nextcloud on projects and tasks
  • Standalone Nextcloud application, and a systray panel that opens at the folder of the record on screen
  • One Nextcloud account per person, connected once, revocable any time
  • Search the whole tree; insert an internal link or a share in a message; list and revoke the shares cited in a chatter
  • Folder tree, sortable columns, inline preview; upload (drag-and-drop, multi-file), folder, rename, move, delete
  • Configurable share presets (internal / external), existing shares shown with their expiry
  • Link files to the Knowledge Matrix

Security by design

Every operation is gated by an access group, scoped to the record's folder, and validated against path traversal. Each person connects their own Nextcloud account through Nextcloud's Login Flow v2, single sign-on included: Nextcloud applies its own permissions, and every share link, copied file and activity entry carries the person's name. App passwords are stored encrypted, never reach the browser, and are revoked on disconnection or when the Odoo account is archived.

On a company without folder, the tab and its buttons appear only once the Nextcloud configuration is usable and has a clients root; the creation button also needs a template.
