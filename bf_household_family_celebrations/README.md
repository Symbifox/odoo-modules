# Household Family: Celebrations (`bf_household_family_celebrations`)

Bridge between `bf_household_family` and `bf_celebrations`. Installs by
itself. LGPL-3. Labels in English, French (Canada) in `i18n/fr_CA.po`.

A child whose birth day is known gets their birthday in Celebrations, like a colleague: the
daily cron posts one « Birthday » occasion on the child's contact within its horizon, with an
all-day calendar entry and the parent who holds the records as organiser. A parent can turn
it off (« Celebrate their birthday »). The age is never computed nor shown.
