# Symbifox École: timetable and school calendar (`bf_school_timetable_calendar`)

Installs by itself when both the timetable (`bf_school_timetable`) and the school calendar
(`bf_school_calendar`) are installed. The office enters each day once, in the school calendar.

## Features

- **The timetable follows the school calendar.** When a school keeps its calendar for a year
  (the calendar holds at least one day of that year), the days of its timetables for that year
  are the days of the calendar between the timetable's dates:
  - a class day of the calendar is a class day of the timetable;
  - a pedagogical day is a pedagogical day; a school break, a holiday or an emergency closure is
    a holiday or break: none of them uses a day of the cycle;
  - the days of the cycle are numbered again after each change.
- **Every change follows**: a day added, removed, moved or changed in the calendar, a timetable
  created, or new dates for a timetable. A published timetable follows too, and so do the
  family's portal and calendar link.
- **Exam days** are still marked in the timetable, on a class day. An exam day stays one while
  the calendar holds a class day on that date; a closure or a holiday that day ends it.
- In a timetable that follows the calendar, the days are not added, removed or moved by hand,
  and a day only changes between class day and exam day: the rest is changed in the calendar.
  The timetable form says so.
- **A year's first days in the calendar take what its timetables hold**, at install and at any
  time after: when the calendar receives the first days of a school year (generated by the
  button, or a single day), it first takes the days of that year's timetables (a published
  timetable first): a date they hold comes in, and a class day just generated takes the
  timetable's kind. A pedagogical day entered in the timetable is never lost. An exam day
  becomes a class day of the calendar (the exam stays in the timetable); a holiday or break
  becomes a holiday, with child care closed. At install, every timetable then reads the
  calendar.
- A school with no calendar for a year keeps its timetable as before: the button makes one class
  day for each weekday, and the days are changed in the timetable.

## What has not been confirmed

- No pilot school yet.
- A break taken from a timetable at install becomes a holiday of the calendar (child care
  closed): the office changes it to a school break where child care opens.
- A calendar emptied of every day of a year frees its timetables: they keep their days and are
  changed by hand again.
