The part templates in open_peeps.json and notionists.json were exported from
the DiceBear packages @dicebear/open-peeps 9.4.2 and @dicebear/notionists 9.4.2
by export.mjs, which writes them as open_peeps.json and notionists.json.

Design: Open Peeps by Pablo Stanley (https://www.openpeeps.com/) and
Notionists by Zoish (https://heyzoish.gumroad.com/l/notionists), both
CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
