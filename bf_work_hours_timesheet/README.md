# BF Working Hours - Timesheets (`bf_work_hours_timesheet`)

Bridge between **BF Working Hours** and **Timesheets**, installed automatically when both are.

Each day shows the hours the person entered beside the hours reconstructed from their traces.
Two numbers for two uses: a timesheet attributes effort to work, the reconstruction measures
presence. There is no alert when one exceeds the other, and neither replaces the other. A colleague the
person shares their days with sees both numbers.
