# Symbifox École: attendance (`bf_school_attendance`)

Roll call, absences declared and justified by the families, and a same-day notice
when a child is missing without a reason.

## Features

- **Mode per group**: morning and afternoon (elementary) or each period (secondary,
  with the number of periods per day). No regulation sets the frequency; this is the
  usual practice.
- **Take attendance**: a teacher opens a roll call for a group they teach. Everyone is
  present by default; the absences the family already declared are pre-filled,
  justified, with their reason. Present, absent or late (with minutes), one click each.
- **Same-day notice**: closing the roll call tells the adults who receive notices
  about every unjustified absence, with a link to justify it. At most once per student
  and day, whatever the number of roll calls.
- **Family portal** (**Absences**): declare an absence to come (days, whole day,
  morning or afternoon, reason, comment) or justify one after the fact. A late
  declaration justifies the roll calls it covers. Older than 30 days or longer than
  30 days: the family calls the office.
- **Office**: absence reasons (justified or not, offered to families or not),
  declarations taken by phone, correction of a closed roll call, and the list of
  students with repeated unjustified absences (3 distinct days or more in 30 days).

## Law

- Compulsory attendance applies to every child living in Québec (Education Act s. 14).
- The process of s. 18 (act with the parents, notify them in writing, then report to
  the Director of Youth Protection) binds public school principals only. In every
  school, any staff member must report under the Youth Protection Act (s. 38-39). The
  list of repeated absences is an aid, not a decision.
- Texts said "an absence for X", never "X is absent": a contact carries no gender.

## Security

A teacher takes and reads the roll calls of the groups they teach. The office sees and
corrects everything. Families have no access right: the portal reads through the
adult's own guardian links.

## What has not been confirmed

- No SMS or push notice yet: email only.
- No per-period timetable: the period number is typed, not read from a schedule.
- No certificate upload for long absences.
