# Symbifox École: conduct and monthly follow-up (`bf_school_conduct`)

Breaches of the rules of conduct, graduated sanctions, parents' notices as the law
frames them, and the monthly news of Régime pédagogique s. 29.2.

## Features

- **Rules of conduct** seeded from the Regulation respecting rules of conduct in
  elementary and secondary schools (I-13.3, r. 10.01), which binds public AND private
  schools since 2025: civility (formal "vous", madame and monsieur), cell phone during
  the school day, image or voice captured without consent, respect, schedules and
  deadlines, bullying, violence, sexual violence. The school adds its own.
- **Sanctions** from r. 10.01 s. 5 (warning, written reflection, device withheld,
  detention, internal and external suspension) and restorative gestures.
- **Breaches**: the count of previous breaches of the same rule this school year and
  the suggested next level (one sanction per level). An aid: a person decides.
- **Informing the parents**, checked on the server:
  - a suspension gives its reasons and the support measures (Education Act s. 96.27,
    Private Education Act s. 63.6);
  - sexual violence from 14 (or with no birth date on file): only with the student's
    consent;
  - an email to the adults who receive notices, and the breach appears in their
    portal (**Follow-up**). A breach the family was not told about never shows.
- **Annual report** on bullying and violence (Private Education Act s. 63.8, due to the
  ministry by December 31): pivot by rule and year.
- **Monthly follow-up** (Régime pédagogique s. 29.2): risk of failure, conduct,
  intervention plan. At the start of each month, a to-do for the responsible teacher
  of every student whose parents got no news the month before. News sent by email
  reaches the family and its portal; a phone call or meeting is only noted.

## Security

A teacher reads and records the breaches and follow-ups of the students they teach
this year; the office sees everything. Families have no access right. Only the button
informs the parents, after checking the conditions (the student's consent for sexual
violence from 14): the date they were informed cannot be written by hand, and a teacher
cannot move a breach to another student. Breaches and monthly news reach the adults
with parental authority, not every adult who receives notices.

## What has not been confirmed

- The bullying and violence plan (Education Act s. 75.1, Private Education Act s. 63.1)
  and the complaint process are not modelled: only the breaches and their notices.
- The parents of OTHER students involved in a bullying case (the victim's, the
  witnesses') are not linked to the breach yet.
- The ministry's format for the annual report was not found.
