# BF Working Hours - Email (`bf_work_hours_email`)

Bridge between **BF Working Hours** and **BF Email**, installed automatically when both are.

An email the person sends from their own mail client, read back from their Sent folder,
counts as a trace of their working time: the five minutes before it. Not to whom, not its text.

Emails sent from the chatter do not count here: they may come from an agent, or be a scheduled
send whose time says nothing about when it was written. Writing them in Odoo already shows as
browser activity.
